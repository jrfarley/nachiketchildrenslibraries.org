Volunteer

In Canada, we are committed to expanding access to the very best learning opportunities for all children.


Here is the novel opportunity for championing right to nourishing learning environment half way across the globe. Remember changing rural India is changing the world.


Earn international experience and at the same time the thrill of being a part of local community made of tribal, rural, and urban librarians committed to improving the lives of children.


Nachiket is a great opportunity to teach conversational skills in English. Children will be thrilled to hear the sounds of Canadian English.


You could also get involved in the following activities:


  • Computer Literacy
  • Arts and Crafts
  • Fun with Science
  • Games
